Two ways to play

Solo or with a group.

Play it yourself

Pick a game, pick a difficulty, go. Nothing to set up and nothing to connect. It's a great way to practice so you come out on top at the next party.

  • Works with no signal
  • Solo difficulty on every game
  • Practice makes perfect

Or with family & friends

Start a game, share the code, and everyone plays the same board on their own device. First to finish takes it. No limit to how many people can join. Make it a party!

  • Everyone works on the same puzzle
  • Laugh and play together
  • On to the next round!

Game 01

Vibe Five

Name three things. You have five seconds.

A category lands on screen — pizza toppings, things in a junk drawer — you have five seconds to name three that fit. The buzzer does not care about your excuses.

  1. Read the category out to the room.
  2. Start the clock when everyone is ready.
  3. Five seconds. Then the buzzer.

Nearly 1,000 categories across 11 packs.

Game 02

Word Vibe

Six guesses. Everyone gets the same word.

A five-letter word, six tries, and colored hints. Play it on your own, or start a group game to see who can find the word the fastest.

  1. Guess a word. Green means right letter, right place.
  2. Amber means it's in there somewhere.
  3. Grey means give up on it.
  4. Solve it before anyone else does.

Thousands of words to discover. Also playable in a browser.

Game 03

Number Vibe

Fill the grid. Beat the room to it.

Proper Sudoku with pencil marks and clash warnings. Play Easy, Medium or Hard on your own — or race your friends to see who can finish first.

  1. Pick a difficulty, then play solo or with a group.
  2. Use notes for pencil marks, tap a number twice to undo it.
  3. Every puzzle is generated fresh. There are billions.

Infinite puzzles, each graded by the technique required to solve it.

Game 04

Vibe Pairings

Let's keep things either the same or different — but not in between.

Twelve charcuterie boards are face up. Every board has a platter, a cheese, a meat and a garnish, and each comes three ways. Three cards make a good pairing when every one of those features is either the same on all three or different on all three — two matching and a third odd is not a proper pairing.

  1. Tap three cards. They light up as you pick them.
  2. Wrong? They flash red and nothing moves. Look again.
  3. Clear the deck solo, or race the room: one shared table, and a good pairing taken by anyone is gone for everyone.

81 charcuterie boards, shuffled — build your perfect pairings.

Game 05

Similar Vibe

Describe it. Don't say it. Then get rid of it.

Two teams and one word that nobody wants to be holding. One person is shown a word and has to get their team to say it without saying it themselves. Get it right and a new word passes to somebody on the other team — and the clock keeps ticking down.

  1. Four players are required, two on a side, each on their own device.
  2. Get the word and it goes to the other team.
  3. When the clock runs out, the point goes to whichever team is not holding it.

The other team can see the word, and has one enormous red BUZZ button. If you say the word, or any part of it, they will BUZZ you and take the point instead. First to seven points wins.
