Two ways to play

Both work with one phone. One of them works with six.

Play it yourself

Pick a game, pick a difficulty, go. Nothing to set up and nothing to connect — perfect for a commute, a queue, or avoiding conversation.

  • Works with no signal
  • Solo difficulty on every game
  • Your score is your business

Or race the room

Start a game, read out the code, and everyone plays the same board on their own phone. First to finish takes it.

  • Same puzzle for everybody
  • Live player list and winner announcement
  • Straight into the next round

Game 01

Vibe Five

Name three things. You have five seconds.

A category lands on screen — pizza toppings, things in a junk drawer — and the table has five seconds to name three that fit. The buzzer does not care about your excuses.

  1. Read the category out to the room.
  2. Start the clock when everyone is ready.
  3. Five seconds. Ticks for the last three. Then the buzzer.

1,100 categories across 11 packs. Pass one phone around.

Game 02

Word Vibe

Six guesses. Everyone gets the same word.

A five-letter word, six tries, and green-amber-grey hints. Play it quietly on your own, or start a game and watch the winner get announced to the whole room.

  1. Guess a word. Green means right letter, right place.
  2. Amber means it is in there somewhere. Grey means give up on it.
  3. Solve it before anyone else does.

14,855 words to guess from. Also playable in a browser.

Game 03

Number Vibe

Fill the grid. Beat the room to it.

Proper Sudoku with pencil marks and clash warnings. Play Easy, Medium or Hard on your own — or pick the difficulty yourself and race friends on the same board, whoever is hosting.

  1. Pick a difficulty, then play solo or share a code to race.
  2. Notes mode for pencil marks, tap a number twice to undo it.
  3. Every puzzle is generated fresh. There are billions.

Infinite puzzles, each graded by the technique it needs — not by how many clues it happens to show.

Game 04

Vibe Pairings

Same on all three, or different on all three.

Twelve charcuterie boards are face up. Every board has a platter, a cheese, a meat and a garnish, and each comes three ways. Three cards make a good pairing when every one of those features is either the same on all three or different on all three — two matching and a third odd is not it.

  1. Tap three cards. They light up as you pick them.
  2. Wrong? They flash red and nothing moves. Look again.
  3. Clear the deck solo, or race the room: one shared table, and a good pairing taken by anyone is gone for everyone.

81 boards, one seed — so every phone in the race rebuilds the identical table, and the only thing sent is which cards were taken.

Game 05

Similar Vibe

Describe it. Don't say it. Then get rid of it.

Two teams and one word that nobody wants to be holding. One person is shown a word and has to get their team to say it without saying it themselves. Get it right and the word passes to somebody on the other team — and the clock does not stop for anybody, or tell anybody how long is left. The ticking speeds up when it is nearly over.

  1. Four players at least, two a side, each on their own phone.
  2. Get the word and it goes to the other team. Nobody scores for guessing.
  3. When the clock runs out, the point goes to whichever team is not holding it — and whoever is left holding it starts the next round.

The other team can see the word, and gets one enormous red button. Say it, or any part of it, and they take the point instead. First to seven.
