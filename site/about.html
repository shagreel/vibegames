About Vibe Games

Vibe Games is six quick party games for a phone, a tablet or a browser. I built them for my own family, and left them here so that other families can play them too.

The six games

  • Vibe Five — name three things before the five-second buzzer, one device passed round the table.
  • Word Vibe — a five-letter word, six guesses, and everyone in the room gets the same one.
  • Number Vibe — Sudoku with pencil marks and clash warnings, played solo or raced to the finish.
  • Vibe Pairings — twelve charcuterie boards, and three cards that have to be either the same all through or different all through.
  • Vibe Ten — nine columns of numbers, pairs that are equal or add to ten, and a board that keeps going while there is room.
  • Similar Vibe — describe the word without saying it, for two teams of two and one very large red button.

Who makes it

I am one person, and these are my family's games. We play them in the evenings — it is how we wind down together — and I wanted to share that with other families rather than keep it to ourselves.

It is free. There is no account to make, nothing to unlock and no adverts, and there is no tracking beyond a page counter. Nobody has to sign in to play, and nobody is selling anything to the people who do.

Where to play it

The browser version is at play.vibegames.party, and it needs nothing installed: no download, no store, no sign-up. Open it on whatever is to hand and pass it round.

There is an iPhone and iPad app as well, on its way to the App Store — the front page carries the link as soon as it is there.

Privacy and support

The privacy page says exactly what is collected, where it goes and how long it is kept, in the same plain terms as this page. The support page is where to write when something is not working, or when a game is missing that ought to exist: the box there reaches me, and there is no email address to go looking for.

That is the whole of it: six games, one person, no charge. If anything here stops being true, this page changes with it.
