Privacy

The games are free, there are no accounts, and there are no adverts. This is everything they collect, and what happens to it.

What is collected

Five things, and nothing else:

  • What you type into the feedback box. The note itself, which app it came from (iPhone or browser), which game you were in when you wrote it, and when.
  • The name you choose. A nickname. It is kept on your device, and written into the game you are playing so the other players can see whose turn it is. It is not an account and it is not tied to anything else.
  • How the games are used, in counts. Which game was started, solo or with others, whether it was finished, the score, how long it took, and which screens were opened. Not the words anybody guessed or described, and nothing else anybody typed.
  • Crash reports. What the app was doing when it broke, with the device model and iOS version, so that a crash can be fixed rather than guessed at.
  • Who is in a game with you. The room you are playing in: its code, its players, and its scores, so that every phone in the room sees the same thing.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising. The analytics in the app are built without the ad ID on purpose, which is also why it never asks permission to track you.
  • Nothing about you from other apps or websites: no location, no contacts, no photos, no camera and no microphone.

Where it goes

All of it goes to Google Firebase, which is what runs the game rooms, the counts and the crash reports. Firebase is operated by Google, and Google's own privacy policy covers how it handles data on this project's behalf. The data is stored on servers in the United States.

Notes sent through the feedback box are published. They arrive in the project's issue tracker — a public repository at github.com/shagreel/vibegames — so that a report can be worked on and closed where it can be seen. Do not put anything private in one.

How long it is kept

  • Game rooms are deleted 24 hours after they are made. The job that does it runs once a day.
  • Feedback is kept until the issue it became has been dealt with, and can be removed on request.
  • Counts and crash reports are kept by Google according to the project's retention setting.
  • The name on your device stays there until you change it or delete the app.

Children

These are family games and children play them. Nothing that identifies anybody is collected: the only thing a child can give is a nickname, which lives in a room that is deleted within a day. There is no advertising and no tracking, so there is nothing to aim at them.

Your choices

  • Play without any of it: the solo games and Vibe Five need no connection at all, and send nothing.
  • Change or clear the name on your device from the settings on the home screen.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it kept locally.
  • Ask for a note you sent to be removed, through the support page.
